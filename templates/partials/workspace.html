Reference Notes

  1. Normative lookup-table structure is modeled around Kyriakopoulou 2017 fetal brain MRI reference centile curves.
  2. Fractional gestational age is evaluated as completed weeks plus days divided by 7 before any analytic or spline-based calculation.
  3. When centile tables are loaded, the 50th centile acts as the expected mean and the 5th to 95th centile spread is converted into an approximate standard deviation under a local normality assumption.
  4. Posterior fossa diameter and clivus-supraocciput angle are validated directly against the Woitek 2014 worked-example math, while the broader spline tables still follow the Kyriakopoulou-style centile schema until source tables are imported in full.
